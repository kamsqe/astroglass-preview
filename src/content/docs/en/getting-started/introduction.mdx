AstroGlass is a premium Astro 6 template designed for startups and creators who want to stand out. It features a unique **Glassmorphism** design system built on top of Tailwind CSS v4, with an interactive CLI configurator that lets you pick your theme, languages, and color palettes — then prunes everything you don't need.

## Why AstroGlass?

- **Premium Design**: 6 complete theme variants with depth and blur effects.
- **10 Color Palettes**: Light, dark, and colorful — switch with a single attribute.
- **CLI Configurator**: Interactive setup that prunes unused themes, locales, and palettes.
- **Full i18n**: Built-in support for 7 languages, easily extensible.
- **Edge-Ready**: Deploy to Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, or Netlify out of the box.

## What's Included?

The template comes with 6 fully designed themes, each providing 12+ page sections (Hero, About, Features, Pricing, Testimonials, FAQ, CTA, Contact, Header, Footer, plus Landing Page sections), a dedicated Portfolio Page, a custom 404 page, plus a blog system and full documentation.
