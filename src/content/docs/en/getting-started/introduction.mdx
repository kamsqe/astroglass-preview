Liquid Glass is a premium Astro theme designed for startups and creators who want to stand out. It features a unique **Glassmorphism** design system built on top of Tailwind CSS.

## Why Liquid Glass?

- **Premium Design**: Ready-to-use components with depth and blur effects.
- **Hybrid Performance**: Statically generated content with server-side capabilities.
- **Developer Experience**: Type-safe content collections and easy configuration.
